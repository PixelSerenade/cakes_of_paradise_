01 / THE DETAILS YOU SHARE

What we collect

We collect information you enter into the site or share with the bakery.

♙

Account & profile

Your name, email address, password and optional phone number. Passwords are hashed before they are saved. If you use account features, you may also save addresses, favourite cakes, a bag, and occasion dates or reminder preferences.

♧

Orders & delivery

Order items and options, contact details, delivery or pickup choice, address and PIN code when needed, requested date and time, notes, payment method and payment status.

✎

Enquiries & messages

Contact messages include the details you enter, such as your name, email and message. A custom cake enquiry may also include your phone, occasion, cake size, design notes and an optional reference image.

♡

Newsletter, gifts & reviews

Newsletter signup stores your email and consent preferences. Gift card orders may include a recipient’s name, email and note. Product reviews save your account name, rating and comment, and appear with the cake you reviewed.

02 / WHY WE USE IT

How we use your information

Each detail supports a part of the service you choose to use.

  • 01

    Run your account and bag. Sign you in, remember your saved cakes and addresses, and show your account details.

  • 02

    Prepare and fulfil orders. Coordinate the cake, payment status, delivery or pickup, and order updates.

  • 03

    Reply to you. Answer custom cake enquiries, contact-form messages, account questions and support requests.

  • 04

    Deliver gifts and chosen updates. Send purchased gift cards, service emails and newsletter messages when you have opted in.

  • 05

    Show product reviews. Display the rating and comment you submit alongside the cake you reviewed.

03 / PAYMENTS & SUPPORTING SERVICES

Payments and service providers

Some parts of the shop rely on services that help us complete the action you requested.

₹

Online payments

Online checkout is handled by Razorpay. Our order records keep payment details such as the selected method, status and provider reference used to verify and update your order.

✉

Order and bakery emails

Email delivery services help send account, order, enquiry and newsletter messages. We share the details needed to deliver the message you requested.

⌘

Storage in your browser

Your browser stores items such as your bag, wishlist, sign-in session and display preferences. Temporary session storage can support checkout and navigation between pages.

♡

Bakery team

Bakery staff use order and enquiry details to prepare orders, coordinate pickup or delivery, answer questions and manage the shop.

✧ We use these details to operate the shop and complete the features you choose to use.

04 / YOUR PREFERENCES

Your choices

You can manage some details directly and contact us for help with the rest.

01

Update account details

Visit My Account or Settings to review profile details, saved addresses and preferences.

02

Change newsletter preferences

Use the unsubscribe link in a bakery email to stop newsletter messages. Order and account emails may still be sent when needed to provide those services.

03

Ask us to review your information

For a correction, removal request or privacy question, contact the bakery through our contact form. Include enough detail for us to find your account or order.
