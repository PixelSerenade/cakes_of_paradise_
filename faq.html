01 / PLACE A SWEET ORDER

Ordering & payment

From the first browse to the final checkout step.

How do I place an order?

Browse the cake collection, choose a cake and any available size or flavour options, then add it to your bag. When you’re ready, continue to checkout and sign in to complete your order.

Which payment methods can I use?

Checkout offers online payment through Razorpay, pay at pickup, and pay on delivery. Any offline payment stays pending until the bakery confirms it.

Can I add a note or message to my cake?

Yes. At checkout, use the optional message field for a cake message or delivery note. For a special design or wording request, include the details in your custom cake enquiry so the bakery can discuss it with you.

Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Please contact the bakery as soon as possible with your order number. Changes depend on how far preparation has progressed, so the bakery will confirm what can be accommodated.

02 / MADE FOR YOUR MOMENT

Custom cakes

Tell us what you’re dreaming up and we’ll take it from there.

How do I request a custom cake?

Visit our Custom Cakes page or send an enquiry through the contact form. Share your occasion, preferred size, design ideas and the date you need it for.

How far ahead should I enquire?

Enquire as early as you can, especially for detailed designs or busy celebration dates. Include your event date so the bakery can check availability and discuss timing.

Can you make a cake for a dietary requirement?

Please contact the bakery before ordering and mention the specific requirement. The team can confirm whether it can be accommodated and explain the ingredients used for the cake you have in mind.

03 / GETTING IT TO YOU

Delivery & pickup

Choose the handoff that suits your celebration.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

Delivery within Surathkal is free. For other addresses, enter your PIN code at checkout to check availability and see any delivery charge before placing the order.

Can I pick up my order from the bakery?

Yes. Select “Pick up from bakery” in checkout. Pickup is currently listed as free.

Can I choose a delivery or pickup time?

Checkout lets you request a date and time. The bakery will confirm the requested time with you.

04 / YOUR ACCOUNT & YOUR CAKE

My account & cakes

Find your order details and get the right answer for your cake.

Where can I check my order status?

Sign in and open My Orders to view your order history and status.

Do I need an account to check out?

Yes. If you’re not signed in when you start checkout, the site will ask you to sign in or create an account before you continue.

How do reward points work?

You earn 1 point for every ₹1 spent on a completed order. Every 100 points is worth ₹1, and points can be redeemed in 100-point steps at checkout, up to the reward limit shown for your order.

How do I redeem a gift card code?

Sign in, open My Account → Redeem a gift card, and enter the secret code from the recipient email. Once accepted, the unused value is added to your gift wallet. Each code can be claimed once.

Where can I use my gift wallet balance?

Your gift wallet is shown in My Account. You can use it toward eligible cake orders at checkout. If you’re buying another gift card, the wallet must cover that gift card’s full price.

Are reward points and gift wallet balance the same?

No. Reward points are earned from completed orders and convert at 100 points = ₹1. Gift wallet balance is rupee value from redeemed gift cards and is tracked separately in your account.

Where can I find ingredient or allergen information?

Check the cake’s details on the shop page for potential allergens. Recipes and cross-contact can vary, so please confirm with the bakery before ordering if you have an allergy or need clarification.

How can I get help with an existing order?

Send a message through our contact page and include your order number so the bakery can find the details.
