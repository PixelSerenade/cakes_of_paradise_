A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Good to know

Quick answers for planning the handoff.Ask us anything  →
How do I check if you deliver to my area?＋

Enter your 6-digit PIN code during checkout. The page will show whether delivery is available and any applicable fee before you place your order.

Is delivery free everywhere?＋

Delivery within Surathkal is free. For other addresses, checkout checks availability and shows any delivery charge for the PIN code you enter.

Can I request a particular delivery or pickup time?＋

Yes. Choose a requested date and time in checkout. The bakery will confirm the requested time with you.

Where can I find the bakery?＋

We’re in Surathkal, Mangaluru, Karnataka. Open the map for directions, or contact us if you need help finding the bakery.

Can I add delivery instructions?＋

Use the optional message field at checkout for a cake message or delivery note. For a custom cake or a special timing request, please contact the bakery early.
