OUR STORY

How It All Began ♡

Cakes of Paradise began in a small home kitchen in Surathkal with a simple belief: every celebration deserves something made with care.

What started as a small passion project grew into a dream to create cakes that are not just desserts, but little works of art. We take inspiration from vintage details, soft colours, and the happiness that comes from sharing a cake with the people you love.

Every cake is made fresh to order, finished by hand, and packed with the same care we would give to our own celebrations.
